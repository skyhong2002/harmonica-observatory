## Durable data and rebuild behavior

- [Public source registry](../data/sources/harmonica-source-watchlist-public.csv): stable IDs, names, geographic association, roles and canonical public links. Keep the same `public_id` when correcting a handle or site; do not create a duplicate artist.
- [Reviewed profile manifest](../data/sources/source-profile-overrides.json): English editorial summaries, tags, exact official image URL and profile URL, short original-bio excerpts when available, supporting evidence, verification date and verified YouTube channel identity. `activityDate` records non-video activity; `upcomingEventStartDate` records an advertised future event. `latestVerifiedUpload` is reserved for a verified upload date and may be absent.
- [Description translations](../data/sources/source-description-translations.json) and [name translations](../data/sources/source-name-translations.json): English, Traditional Chinese, Japanese and Korean text tied to stable source IDs. Keep summaries aligned with the reviewed profile if the source description changes; preserve official personal names.
- [Tracked source images](../assets/source-avatars-curated/): the maintained WebP assets. Filenames use the first 20 hexadecimal characters of SHA-256 of the exact remote image URL. Image availability on an official page establishes provenance; it is not a claim of an open reuse license.
- [Profile application helper](../scripts/curated_source_profiles.py): validates stable identity and the image filename, copies the tracked image to `site/assets/source-avatars/`, and applies the reviewed summary/tags during [public-data generation](../scripts/build_public_data.py). These profiles therefore survive loss of local collection caches.
- [Social-source builder](../scripts/build_social_sources.py): accepts a reviewed YouTube channel ID only when the source name and canonical channel URL still match the registry. Revalidate the account relationship before changing a channel handle or ID.

For a correction, update the canonical registry/profile/translation inputs and tracked image as applicable, run the repository’s usual rebuild and validations, then inspect the generated directory/source page. Editing only generated `site/` files or private caches is not a durable correction. Verification dates should be refreshed only after actually checking the cited sources again.

## Research limitations and identity safeguards

Only David Barrett, Tomlin Leckie and Chris Maunders had explicit full-time wording in the reviewed official biographies. Other professional descriptions are supported by teaching, touring, recording, artist or booking pages; they do not establish full-time employment. Håkan Ehn explicitly describes harmonica as a hobby, while Rohan Singhal is included as a performer without an employment claim. Nita Das is a directory/teaching source with no verified recent social cadence.

Manufacturer, festival, school and teaching-platform biographies are attributed editorial evidence, not automatically first-person self-descriptions. In particular, Franco Luciani, Pablo Fagundes, Dave Ferguson and Nita Das lacked an accessible self-written social bio in this research. Their summaries are editorial; do not fill empty original-bio fields with invented quotations. A quote field’s source URL should be read to determine authorship.

Channel identity requires cross-links or embedded-video author evidence. Subham Mukherjee’s verified account is `@subhamsinstrumental5152`; similarly named `@SoundBazz` and `@SubhamMukherjee` accounts were excluded. Max De Aloe’s verified account is `@ChromaticHarmonica1` (channel `UCLYKnK8gjvY8RvR7MvN5Kuw`), not `@ChromaticHarmonica`. Mitch Grainger’s channel title used “Granger” at verification; the artist’s canonical spelling remains Grainger.

Rohan Singhal’s unavailable personal YouTube link was omitted. His music website was also omitted from the public registry after an unrelated offscreen casino link was observed; verified Instagram/Facebook links remain. The artist site is retained only as biography/photo provenance. This observation does not establish the cause of the unrelated link. Justin Brady’s embedded videos belonged to collaborators/fans, and Chris Maunders’ school accounts were program accounts; neither was presented as a verified personal YouTube account. Old artist domains for Franco Luciani, Pablo Fagundes and Greg Zlap were unavailable, so usable official artist/teaching/social evidence was retained instead. Restore a domain only after checking it again.

Portraits were taken from verified official profiles, artist sites, or their professional teaching/artist pages and checked for subject identity. Dave Ferguson’s selected SoundCloud avatar is a harmonica tattoo, not a face portrait; this accurately preserves the account image. Some artist photographs include instruments or another musician in the background. Do not substitute a lookalike, generated portrait or unrelated channel’s avatar.

Geography is intentionally qualified where evidence is limited: Mitch Grainger has Australian origins and a US career, without a confirmed current residence; Ariel Bart’s Berlin association is an operating base. Future events for Todd Parrott, Sébastien Charlier and Will Pound are leads for follow-up, not proof of performances already completed. This document does not assert deployment or live-feed health; those require the separate publication and runtime checks.
